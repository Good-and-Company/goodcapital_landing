Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · The Good Project, LLC and Good & Company Missouri LLC ("we")

Scope

This policy covers thegoodproject.net and the internal accounting connector ("QBO Connector") we use with QuickBooks Online for our own books.

What we collect

  • Website: standard analytics (Google Analytics) and anything you send via the contact form.
  • QBO Connector: accounting data from our own QuickBooks Online companies only (transactions, invoices, vendors, customers). It is used solely by our staff and bookkeepers to keep our books. No third-party company data is accessed.

How we use it

To run our organization, keep accurate books, file taxes, and respond to you. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

Only with service providers needed to operate (hosting, analytics, Intuit QuickBooks, our bookkeeper and CPA), and when required by law.

Security and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on our own systems with restricted access. Accounting records are retained as required by tax law (generally 7 years).
